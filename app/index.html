Roadmap to the platinum

Three steps, about 200 hours. No difficulty setting exists, cheats disable trophies and saving, there is no mission replay, and there are 15 manual save slots plus one autosave — keep a save at the start and end of each chapter.

Step 1 — Finish the story, and do the side content as it appears 60–80 hours

Play the story through and enjoy it. The 100% checklist is not visible until after the story, but any progress you make during the story already counts toward it, so scan vehicles, study animals and take optional missions as they appear. There are no mission gold medals to worry about. Two things matter in this step and nothing else does: meditate at Jason's house in Chapter 1 (the one missable trophy — after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept), and keep a save at the start and end of each chapter, rotating the remaining slots. Quest givers sometimes go quiet for a chapter and fences sometimes disappear for good, but every trophy-related character comes back, so do not worry about the rest.

Step 2 — The 100% Completion Checklist and 70 of 80 challenges about 100 hours

After the story, the checklist opens under Progress, Stats, 100% Completion. Despite the name it is closer to 60% true completion: most side quests are not required at all, and most collectible types are only partly required. The headline numbers are 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 base-model vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, and any 70 of the 80 challenges. The only categories that demand everything are the robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret's hunting quests. Nothing here is missable and everything is doable in free-roam, so work down the list at your own pace.

Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and the last miscellaneous trophies about 10 hours

By now most trophies are already earned. Strangers & Freaks strands are not needed for 100%, so clean up the eight that count (Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete), then sweep the remaining miscellaneous trophies — the UFO, the bike kicks, the cocktail, the showroom Banshee — and the platinum is yours.
